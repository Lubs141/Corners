Match combined

Every home fixture is combined with every away fixture — Home's home-ground record against its various opponents, matched against Away's away-ground record against its various opponents — to model all the ways this specific matchup could play out.

This treats Home's home form and Away's away form as statistically independent of each other — the same simplifying assumption standard scoreline models use. It's a reasonable starting point for a projection, not a guarantee of how the match will actually unfold.
